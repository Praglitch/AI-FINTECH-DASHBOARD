# AI-Powered Stock Analysis Platform

## Overview

Analyze listed companies using financial data, market information, corporate actions, announcements, and AI-generated insights.

The platform combines structured stock market data with local LLM-powered analysis to help users understand companies faster and make more informed investment decisions.

---

## Key Features

### Company Research

* Company Search
* Company Profile
* Industry Information

### Market Intelligence

* Market Snapshot
* Price Information
* Trading Data

### Financial Analysis

* Financial Data
* Key Metrics
* Historical Information

### Corporate Insights

* Shareholding Pattern
* Corporate Actions
* Company Announcements

### AI-Powered Analysis

* Business Overview
* Company Strengths
* Risk Analysis
* Investor Takeaways
